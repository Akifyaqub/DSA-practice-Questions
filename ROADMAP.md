# DSA Roadmap

My plan for learning Data Structures & Algorithms — 3–5 problems a day, ~4 months.

## Phase 1 — Basics (Weeks 1–2)
Time & space complexity, arrays, strings. 25 problems.

## Phase 2 — Searching & Sorting (Weeks 3–4)
Binary search + variants, sorting, two pointers, sliding window. 30 problems.

## Phase 3 — Recursion & Backtracking (Week 5)
Recursion patterns, subsets, permutations, N-Queens. 15 problems.

## Phase 4 — Linked List, Stack, Queue (Weeks 6–7)
Reversal, cycle detection, next greater element. 25 problems.

## Phase 5 — Trees & BST (Weeks 8–9)
Traversals, height, diameter, BST operations. 25 problems.

## Phase 6 — Hashing, Heap & Graphs (Weeks 10–12)
Hash maps, heaps, BFS/DFS, shortest path. 30 problems.

## Phase 7 — Dynamic Programming (Weeks 13–16)
Knapsack, LCS, LIS, grid DP. 30 problems.

## Phase 8 — Revision & Interviews (ongoing)
Redo marked problems, mock interviews, GFG/LeetCode contests.

## How I solve each problem
1. Read it
2. Think for 20 minutes
3. Stuck? Look at the hint only
4. Code it
5. Dry run by hand
6. Couldn't solve it? Mark it and redo it after 3 days

## Daily routine
- 30 min on the topic
- 1–2 hours solving problems
- Post what I learned on LinkedIn
